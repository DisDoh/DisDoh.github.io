SECTION 02

About the App

Clean QR and URL is a mobile utility for scanning QR codes, extracting web addresses from pasted text, and simplifying URLs. It is designed to reduce common tracking parameters and unnecessary redirects and to encourage HTTPS where possible.

Actual results depend on each URL and website. The App does not promise to identify, block or eliminate every tracker, redirect, malicious website or other online risk.

SECTION 04

URL Cleaning and HTTPS

Removing tracking parameters or following a cleaner URL may change how some websites work, including sign-in links, shopping carts, referral codes, or other links that depend on URL parameters. Enforcing or preferring HTTPS does not guarantee that the destination is trustworthy or that HTTPS is available on every website.

You should check the destination and any important information before submitting data, signing in, downloading files or making payments.
